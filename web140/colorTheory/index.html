Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

Explain how your color choices relate to color theory and how you would use each palette in a website.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: I selected these colors because they're some of my favorites. I wanted to find a good-looking combination of blue and gold.

Mood: I think blue gives off a calm vibe, while gold usually represents status or class. Combining the two creates a professional mood that can inspire focus.

Website Usage: For an actual website, I would use a brighter blue as the primary color for banners and headers because blue is often associated with calm and professionalism. I would use the accent blue to complement and add depth to the brighter blue, preventing the design from feeling to flat. Gold would be used more selectively for important content, highlights, and accents.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: I chose these colors because I wanted a fun looking analogous palette. A lot of the color combinations ended up looking dark and boring. Instead i decided to use pinks, reds, and purples to create a more interesting and lively color scheme.

Mood: This palette gives me a creative and artistic vibe. The purples and reds work well together and give the color scheme a more feminine feel.

Website Usage: I would use the primary color for the banner because, out of all the colors, it stands out the most when surrounded by the secondary and accent colors. I would use the accent colors for dividers and lines throughout the page. The secondary color could be used for section headers because it pairs well with the red text.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I wanted to create a more diverse blue palette compared to the complimentary palette. I used secondary colors that fell between two other colors, which helped create more variety. This allowed me to make a nice and unique palette.

Mood: This color combination gives off a calm but fun vibe. The green and blue work well together to create a lively and refreshing mood.

Website Usage: For an actual website, I would use the primary blue for most of the site. The secondary green would work well for headers and dividers. Since the accent color is red, I think it would make buttons stand out and draw attention.
